# Data Dictionary

## 1. Customers Dataset

Grain: One customer record associated with an order.

Primary Key: customer_id

Business Entity Identifier: customer_unique_id

Important distinction:
customer_id identifies the customer record,
while customer_unique_id identifies the actual
customer across multiple orders.

Use customer_unique_id for repeat customer analysis.

Purpose:
Analyze customer geography and purchasing behaviour.

## 2. ## Orders Dataset

File: olist_orders_dataset.csv

Grain: One row per order.

Primary Key: order_id

Foreign Key: customer_id

Columns:
- order_id
- customer_id
- order_status
- order_purchase_timestamp
- order_approved_at
- order_delivered_carrier_date
- order_delivered_customer_date
- order_estimated_delivery_date

Purpose:
Analyze order volume, sales trends,
order status and delivery performance.

## 3. ## Order Items Dataset

File: olist_order_items_dataset.csv

Grain: One row per product line within an order.

Composite Key:
order_id + order_item_id

Foreign Keys:
- order_id
- product_id
- seller_id

Columns:
- order_id
- order_item_id
- product_id
- seller_id
- shipping_limit_date
- price
- freight_value

Purpose:
Analyze merchandise revenue,
freight charges and product performance.

## 4. Payments Dataset

File: olist_order_payments_dataset.csv

Grain: One payment record.

Composite Key:
order_id + payment_sequential

Important Columns:
- payment_type
- payment_installments
- payment_value

Purpose:
Analyze payment methods and payment values.

## 5. Reviews Dataset

File: olist_order_reviews_dataset.csv

Grain: One review record.

Primary Key: review_id

Important Columns:
- review_id
- order_id
- review_score
- review_comment_title
- review_comment_message

Purpose:
Analyze customer satisfaction.

## 6. Products Dataset

File: olist_products_dataset.csv

Grain: One product.

Primary Key: product_id

Important Columns:
- product_id
- product_category_name
- product_weight_g
- product_length_cm
- product_height_cm
- product_width_cm

Purpose:
Analyze product categories and product characteristics.

## 7. Sellers Dataset

File: olist_sellers_dataset.csv

Grain: One seller.

Primary Key: seller_id

Important Columns:
- seller_id
- seller_zip_code_prefix
- seller_city
- seller_state

Purpose:
Analyze seller geography and performance.

## 8. Geolocation Dataset

File: olist_geolocation_dataset.csv

Grain: One geographic record.

Important Columns:
- geolocation_zip_code_prefix
- geolocation_lat
- geolocation_lng
- geolocation_city
- geolocation_state

Purpose:
Support geographic analysis.

Important:
Postal code prefixes are not necessarily unique.

## 9. Category Translation Dataset

File: product_category_name_translation.csv

Grain: One category translation.

Key: product_category_name

Important Columns:
- product_category_name
- product_category_name_english

Purpose:
Translate Portuguese product categories into English.
